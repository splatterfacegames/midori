Why a generator and not a static pack

One TOML file.
Every variant & LOD.

Static vegetation libraries lock a scene into a handful of fixed silhouettes, mismatched texel densities, and hand-authored LOD steps that break apart when you need curved courtyard hedges, steep top-down RTS readability, or twenty distinct oaks along one street.

Midori keeps the entire botanical description in a human-readable TOML document validated by JSON Schema. Changing the 64-bit seed produces an uncorrelated specimen with identical material conventions, wind-pivot encoding, triangle budgets, and `MSFT_lod` structure—whether you call `midori` in a headless CI build, sculpt interactively in the desktop app, or export straight from the browser.

What Midori builds

Six systems.
One shared core.

Every output comes out of `midori-core` in Rust, compiled natively for the CLI and C FFI and to WebAssembly for the interactive workbench.

01

Hero trees, palms & top-down crowns

Extended Weber–Penn recursive branching with root buttress flares, reaction-wood bow, phototropic sweep, dichotomous desert yuccas, continuous 3D gullwing-cambered palm frond ribbons, and dedicated layered silhouettes for steep isometric ARPG and RTS cameras.

  • Oak · Maple · Ginkgo · Linden
  • Pine · Cypress · Willow · Palm
  • Top-down presets
02

Architectural topiary, vines & florals

Signed-distance sheared boxwood hedges (straight dividers, curved terrace arcs, globes), wall-climbing English ivy networks, potted Strelitzia paddle clumps, and Parisian café planters & hanging cascades built from 3D-cupped 5-petal Viola corollas and herringbone leaf garlands.

  • Lumberyard Bistro suite
  • SDF shear hulls
  • 3D floral corollas
03

Procedural bark & botanical cards

Synthesizes tileable bark heightfields, OpenGL normal maps, and 1024px anti-aliased leaf, conifer-scale, boxwood-sprig, and 2×2 single-organ floral atlases with RGB alpha dilation—or accepts host-provided PNG overrides.

04

Tiled nature patches & scatter

Generates complete groundcover packages (Susuki pampas, alpine, flowering meadow, forest floor, arid scrub): 16-bit terrain heightfields, moss/wetness/crack splat masks, groundcover LOD meshes, and chunked midori.scatter.bin.v1 instances.

05

Automatic LOD chains & impostors

Builds 2-to-5 level MSFT_lod chains from 65k-triangle hero crowns down through area-compensated canopy cards to 500-triangle two-view orthographic crown impostors rasterized at export time.

06

Pivot Painter 2.0 & engine importers

Packs normalized branch/leaf hierarchy depth, wind phase, and stiffness into TEXCOORD_1 and COLOR_0, paired with validated import pipelines for Godot 4 / Tarragon, Unity, and Unreal Engine 5.

How an asset is built

Five deterministic stages.

Every generator—woody Weber–Penn trees, SDF topiary, climbing vines, floral cascades, and biome nature patches—runs through the same reproducible pipeline with configurable stem, leaf, vertex, and triangle budgets.

  1. 01
    Species TOML & Seed

    Parse and schema-validate growth rules, crown shape, ornamental hulls, and LOD targets alongside a 64-bit seed.

    species.schema.json · PCG64
  2. 02
    Woody Skeleton & Hulls

    Grow recursive stems, flared root buttresses, crooked shear-interior rootstocks, or catenary vine streamers.

    Weber–Penn · SDF · Vines
  3. 03
    3D Foliage & Floral Organs

    Emit outward-cupped canopy cards, gullwing palm ribbons, tropical paddles, or 3D zygomorphic flower corollas with baked cushion AO.

    Cards · Ribbons · Corollas
  4. 04
    Procedural PBR Maps

    Synthesize bark height/albedo/normal maps and dilated cutout cards deterministically from the species texture seed.

    1024px PNG · Alpha-dilated
  5. 05
    LODs, Impostors & glTF 2.0

    Build every LOD level, rasterize distant 2-view crown impostors, and pack binary .glb or engine nature packages.

    MSFT_lod · GLB · Scatter v1

Command-line quickstart

# Generate a textured 4-LOD oak, a curved Bistro boxwood hedge, and a 24 m pampas meadow package
midori generate -s presets/species/oak.toml --seed 42 --lod-preset species --textures -o oak.glb
midori generate -s presets/species/bistro_curved_hedge.toml --seed 42 --lod-preset species --textures -o hedge.glb
midori nature -p presets/nature/pampas_meadow.toml -o pampas_package
